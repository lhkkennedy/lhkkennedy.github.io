02QMUL · NLP for Finance

WSJ Sentiment → Cross-Sectional Alpha

Mapped roughly 146,000 Wall Street Journal headlines to S&P 500 firms, classified financial sentiment and converted the signal into a cross-sectional stock-selection strategy.

KEY READ

Compared TF-IDF + PCA + logistic regression against FinBERT, then evaluated the signal out-of-sample with factor alpha, Sharpe and drawdown diagnostics.

Systematic Strategy Allocation Review

Institutional-style due diligence on a decade-long live strategy track record against equity and macro CTA benchmarks, with regime, tail-risk and factor attribution analysis.

KEY READ

04QMUL · Machine Learning

Credit Risk Under Decision Costs

Built leakage-safe credit default pipelines and compared logistic regression, random forests and XGBoost under class imbalance and economically meaningful decision thresholds.

KEY READ

Optuna-tuned XGBoost reached 0.7195 AUC; explicit threshold optimisation materially improved the simplified expected-profit objective.
