01MSc Dissertation · Quant Research

Deep Hedging SPX Straddles

Observable-only, cost-aware neural hedging of near-ATM SPX straddles across five maturity buckets, testing simulation pretraining, market learning and maturity sharing under Heston and lifted-Heston source models.

KEY READ

Final all-bucket point estimate at 1 bp: MSE 0.154984 versus 0.185212 for Black–Scholes delta, a 16.3% reduction. The dissertation reports this as a candidate risk-control effect, not adjusted proof of an edge.

02Independent · Market Microstructure

Quote-Replenishment Hysteresis

Event-conditioned Level-1 study testing whether the path of displayed-liquidity recovery after a depletion shock adds predictive information beyond the endpoint best-book snapshot.

KEY READ

Across 11,874 OOS forecasts, the recovery-path model lowers pooled log loss by 1.15% and wins all four chronological folds; continuation rises from 66.8% to 95.5% across SRA deciles.

03QMUL · NLP for Finance

WSJ Sentiment → Cross-Sectional Alpha

Mapped roughly 146,000 Wall Street Journal headlines to S&P 500 firms, classified financial sentiment and converted the signal into a cross-sectional stock-selection strategy.

KEY READ

Compared TF-IDF + PCA + logistic regression against FinBERT, then evaluated the signal out-of-sample with factor alpha, Sharpe and drawdown diagnostics.
